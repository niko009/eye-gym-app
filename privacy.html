Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 12, 2025

Eye Gym Web App stores your progress and settings only on this device. We do not collect, track, log, or share your personal data.

1. Data Collection

Eye Gym Web App does not collect any personal information. We do not:

  • Track your usage or behavior
  • Collect analytics or telemetry
  • Store data on external servers
  • Use cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • Share any information with third parties

2. Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only using browser localStorage:

  • Language preference: Automatically detected from your Telegram settings
  • Exercise progress: Which exercises you've completed
  • Settings: Reminder preferences, voice guidance preference, premium status

This data never leaves your device and is only accessible to this Web App running in your Telegram app.

3. Telegram Integration

Eye Gym Web App communicates with @EyeGymBot only for the following purposes:

  • Reminders: If you opt in to daily reminders, your preference is sent to the bot to schedule notifications
  • Premium codes: If you enter a premium code, it's validated by the bot

The bot does not store your Telegram user ID or any personal information beyond what's necessary to send scheduled reminders (if enabled).

4. Third-Party Services

Eye Gym Web App uses only one external service:

  • Telegram WebApp SDK (loaded from https://telegram.org/js/telegram-web-app.js) - Required for the app to function within Telegram

No other third-party scripts, analytics, or tracking services are used.

5. Premium Features

Premium exercise unlocks are handled via user-entered codes only. We do not process payments directly in the app. If you choose to support the project via external donation platforms (Boosty, GitHub Sponsors), their respective privacy policies apply.

6. Data Deletion

To delete all your local data:

  1. Close the Eye Gym Web App
  2. Clear your Telegram cache or browser data for this Web App

This will permanently remove all exercise progress, settings, and preferences stored locally.

7. Children's Privacy

Eye Gym Web App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences experiencing digital eye strain.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Your Rights

Since Eye Gym Web App does not collect or store personal data on external servers, there is no personal data to access, export, or delete from our end. All data is stored locally on your device and under your control.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Eye Gym Web App, please contact us:

  • Telegram Bot: @EyeGymBot
  • GitHub Issues: eye-gym-app/issues
